1. A bounded system is the outermost isolated system containing everything and admitting no exchange across its boundary.
2. A bounded system is distinguished from the null system by the presence of nodes.
3. Every node has a static intrinsic character sufficient to distinguish its existence from nonexistence, but insufficient to distinguish it intrinsically from any other node.
4. A directed edge permits, but does not require, communication from its source node to its target node. Any state change or interaction between nodes can occur only through such communication.
5. Directed edges must not form directed cycles.
