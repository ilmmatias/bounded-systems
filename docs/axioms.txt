1. A bounded system is the outermost isolated system containing everything and admitting no exchange across its boundary.
2. A bounded system is distinguished from the null system only by the presence of nodes.
3. Every node has an intrinsic character sufficient to distinguish its existence from nonexistence, but insufficient to distinguish it intrinsically from any other node.
4. Any transference of state, influence, amongst others that could be used to distinguish between between nodes can occur only through directed communication channels we'll call edges.
5. Directed edges must not form directed cycles.
